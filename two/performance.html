Movement & Stage Direction

Performance Art & Choreography

Exploring body movement as living architecture. Fusing ancestral West African polyrhythms with athletic contemporary dance and dramatic stage fog lighting.

"Movement is code in four dimensions. The body compiles emotional truth faster than words can articulate."

With over a decade directing dance ensembles across West Africa and internationally, my choreography investigates memory, resilience, structural rhythm, and human connection on stage.
