Haute Couture & Textiles

Fashion Design & Wearable Art

Honoring indigenous West African textile heritage through structural tailoring, geometric silhouetting, and futuristic tech functionalism.

"Textiles are living history books. By merging hand-loomed Igbo Akwete geometric weaves with sharp cybernetic coats, we re-arm traditional craft for the 22nd century."

Each piece in the collection is crafted in collaboration with master weavers in Abia State, Nigeria, paired with high-precision modern tailoring.
