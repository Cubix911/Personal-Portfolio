Founder's Note

Why This Fund Exists

"Having spent 15+ years in global software engineering, I have met brilliant young minds in Nigeria whose tech journeys were abruptly cut short by unexpected tuition hikes, broken laptops, or lack of internet data."

This fund is designed to step in early—during Year 1 through Year 3—when financial pressure is highest, giving students the security, hardware, and senior mentorship needed to graduate and launch world-class tech careers.

The Four Support Pillars

We do not just hand out grants; we provide a holistic launchpad for student success.

Tuition Relief Grants

Direct semester tuition grants paid to university bursaries to prevent dropouts due to unpaid fees.

Hardware & Laptops

Provision of durable high-performance developer laptops equipped with modern software environments.

Internet Data Stipends

Monthly high-speed data allowances so students can access online documentation, tutorials, and GitHub.

Mentorship & Placement

Dedicated 1:1 code reviews, career guidance, and internship fast-track connections with African tech companies.
